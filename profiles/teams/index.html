Teams deck

A 32-key Stream Deck XL layout for Microsoft Teams on the Mac. Every binding was read from the macOS keymap inside the Teams app itself, the set its code picks when isMac is true. Meeting controls are on top, then calls, reactions and presence status, then chat. Tap a key to see Teams' own command name and when it works.

In the meeting

The Teams key, plus mute, camera, raise hand, share, background blur, live captions, and leave.
